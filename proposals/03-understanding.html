Understanding of Your Requirements

You are building a comprehensive real estate sales and operations platform that replaces the patchwork of spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, disconnected portal logins, and manual follow-ups that most developers still rely on. Based on your requirement document, the system covers five deeply connected pillars.

Pillar 1 — Lead Intelligence & Orchestration

The platform must act as a single intake point for every lead source your business touches — Facebook, Instagram, Google Search, MagicBricks, 99Acres, Housing.com, and CommonFloor. Beyond just capturing leads, it needs to be smart about them -

  • Anti-duplication - when the same buyer inquires through two different portals, the system must merge the inquiries into one timeline instead of creating two competing tickets that two different agents chase.
  • Dynamic lead scoring - a behavioral scoring engine that adds or subtracts points based on real actions (site visit, email open, call outcome) and uses that score to drive the daily calling list, not just most-recent-first.
  • Intelligent routing - skill-based routing so premium inventory goes to senior closers, language-based routing so regional buyers get native speakers.
  • Ghost-call prevention - a 15-minute accountability clock on every assigned lead, so leads do not sit in an agent's queue untouched while the buyer goes cold.

Pillar 2 — Inventory, Pricing & Visual Assets

Your inventory module needs to behave like a living stacking plan, not a static Excel sheet -

  • Interactive stacking plan showing floor-by-floor unit status, with click-to-view details on facing, PLC, floor rise, and the actual balcony view.
  • Soft-block hold management with automated 24-hour expiry timers so managers can reserve units for hot prospects without manually tracking expiry.
  • Complex quote engine that calculates base price, car parking charges, club membership, electricity and water deposits, and legal fees — all in one click.
  • Discount approval workflow that pushes real-time approve/reject decisions to the Director's desk when an agent offers anything above 2%.
  • Centralized asset repository for brochures, walkthrough videos, and RERA certificates, with one-click WhatsApp sharing.
