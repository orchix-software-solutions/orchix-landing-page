Telephony - Click-to-Call, Shadowing, Whisper

Using Exotel or an equivalent provider, we implement -

  • Virtual DID per project — each project gets its own inbound number, so when a call comes in, the CRM knows which project it is about before anyone picks up.
  • Click-to-call from the CRM — agent clicks a lead, telephony provider bridges the two numbers, call is recorded and logged automatically.
  • Live call shadowing — managers see a live list of ongoing calls on their dashboard, can silently join any call as a listener. Supported through compatible telephony providers, subject to plan capability and applicable call-monitoring rules.
  • Whisper coaching — managers can join a live call in one-way audio mode, speaking to the agent while the buyer cannot hear them. Supported through compatible telephony providers, subject to plan capability and applicable call-monitoring rules.

Complex Quote Engine

The quote engine is configuration-driven — you define the price components once per project (base price per sqft, PLC percentages, car parking charges, club membership, preferential location charges, electricity/water/sewage deposits, registration, stamp duty, GST, legal fees) and the engine calculates the quote for any selected unit.

Discount Approval Workflow

Any discount above your configured threshold (e.g., 2%) triggers an in-app notification and email to the approver (Director by default, configurable per project). The lead/quote is locked until the approver responds. Approvals, rejections, and the reasoning are all logged in the audit trail.

Construction-Linked Billing

This is one of the most valuable automations in the system. Site engineers mark project milestones complete in the web app ('Excavation Complete,' 'Foundation Complete,' '1st Floor Slab,' etc.). Each milestone is linked to a billing schedule (typically 10 to 15 percent of total value per milestone). When a milestone is marked complete, a BullMQ job fans out — it pulls every buyer linked to that project, generates a personalized demand letter PDF with their outstanding balance, sends it via email and WhatsApp, logs the demand in their ledger, and sets a payment due date. Hundreds of demand letters go out in minutes instead of days.
