Pillar 3 — The Automation Heavy-Lift

This is where the system stops being a database and starts being a force multiplier for your sales team -

  • WhatsApp drip campaigns — 10-day automated nurture sequences for cold leads that keep warming them up while agents focus on hot ones.
  • Event-triggered messages — auto-sending the site location pin 60 minutes before a visit, and auto-requesting feedback 30 minutes after the visit ends.
  • Virtual DID numbers for every project, so marketing attribution is accurate and agents never expose personal numbers.
  • Click-to-call directly from the CRM so agents stop manually dialing.
  • Call shadowing and whisper coaching so managers can silently listen to live calls, or coach agents one-way without the buyer hearing. Supported through compatible telephony providers, subject to plan capability and applicable call-monitoring rules.

Pillar 4 — Post-Sale, Revenue & Channel Partners

Booking is only the beginning. The platform must handle everything that comes after -

  • Construction-linked billing — when the site engineer updates 'Foundation Complete,' the system fires off demand letters to every linked buyer simultaneously.
  • Bank reconciliation — auto-matching incoming NEFT/RTGS payments with the correct buyer's ledger, so finance stops doing detective work every morning.
  • Channel Partner ecosystem with a 60-day direct lead shield that locks a registered lead to the broker who brought it, ending the endless 'who owns this lead' fights.
  • Live broker payout tracker showing brokerage percentage, total sales, and payment status in real time.
  • OCR-assisted document processing that extracts PAN and Aadhaar details to auto-fill booking forms, with manual review for low-confidence fields, reducing the typing errors that cause registration delays.

Pillar 5 — Management Information Systems (MIS)

Finally, the layer that turns raw activity into decisions -

  • Attribution modeling — first-touch (which ad created awareness), last-touch (which ad drove the booking), and uplift (marketing spend vs. actual banked revenue).
  • Agent productivity audits — talk-time, lead-to-visit conversion ratios, visit-to-booking conversion ratios, all sliced per agent.
