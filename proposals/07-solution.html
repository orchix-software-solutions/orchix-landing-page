3.4 How the Critical Pieces Actually Work

Anti-Duplication & Lead Fingerprinting — Every incoming lead, from any source, hits a normalization service first. The service extracts a fingerprint (normalized phone, normalized email, normalized name) and queries existing records. If a match exists, the new inquiry becomes a timeline event on the existing lead instead of creating a duplicate ticket. The original assigned agent keeps the lead. The new source is logged as an additional touchpoint, which also feeds the multi-touch attribution model later.

Dynamic Lead Scoring — A dedicated BullMQ worker listens to every event in the system — 'site visit logged,' 'email opened,' 'call marked not reachable,' 'feedback received.' Each event has a configured score delta that you control from the management interface. The worker recalculates the lead's score in real time and updates a materialized view that powers the Daily Calling List. The list is always sorted by current score, not by date, so your agents are always working the hottest leads first.

Ghost-Call Prevention — When a lead is assigned to an agent, a BullMQ delayed job is scheduled for 15 minutes in the future. If the agent logs a call attempt before that, the job is cancelled. If they do not, the job fires — the lead is automatically reassigned
