Bank Reconciliation

Incoming payments are reconciled automatically using a match engine that looks at amount matching (expected demand vs. received), UTR/transaction reference matching (where the buyer quoted a reference), name matching (fuzzy match between bank statement name and buyer name), and date proximity. High-confidence matches are auto-applied to the buyer's ledger. Ambiguous matches land in a review queue where finance resolves them with one click. The reconciliation engine stores confirmed mappings and uses them to improve future match suggestions.

Channel Partner Access

Brokers log into the same web app under the Channel Partner role and see a dedicated dashboard. They can register a lead (which locks it to them for 60 days — direct lead shield), see their active leads and current stage of each, view a live brokerage tracker showing earned, pending, and paid amounts, access marketing collateral and RERA certificates, and download commission statements. The 60-day lock is enforced at the database level. If a lead already exists under another broker or in-house agent, registration is blocked with a clear message.

MIS & Executive Dashboard

Reports are powered by a reporting database layer that runs heavy aggregation queries off the main transactional data. Dashboards include attribution (first-touch, last-touch, and full-funnel), marketing ROI (spend per source vs. banked revenue per source), agent productivity (calls made, talk-time, site visits generated, bookings closed, conversion ratios at each stage), inventory velocity (units sold per week per project/wing/floor, with trend lines), and cash flow projection (expected collections over the next 30/60/90 days based on construction milestones and payment schedules).
